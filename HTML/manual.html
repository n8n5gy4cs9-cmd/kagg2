Manual

Reference for everything in Kagg2 v0.35. For a guided start see the player guide.

Running and options#

./run.sh                    normal game (main menu)
./run.sh fight [sector]     jump into a sector with enemies attacking (default mars)
./run.sh dock  [sector]     start docked at the first friendly starbase
./run.sh board [1-4] [size] boarding level: 1 corvette 2 station 3 prison barge 4 dreadnought, size 1-5
./run.sh tab N [sector]     open station tab N (1 helm .. 7 map)
./run.sh sector NAME        fly a sector: mars phobos belt jupiter luna earth
./run.sh viewer [model]     model viewer
./run.sh test               fmt check, clippy, tests
./run.sh shot OUT.png [..]  save a screenshot and exit

These developer shortcuts start free flight without a mission. Extra flags for the binary: --chase, --target, --fire, --throttle F, --pos x,y,z --look x,y,z, --frames N. Environment: KAGG2_HOME (where settings and saves go), KAGGA_FPS=1 (FPS counter), KAGGA_DEV=1 (F9 completes the current mission).

Developer console#

Press ` (or F12) in any screen to open the console; the game pauses while it is open. Commands: help, god [on|off], heal, ammo (torpedoes/missiles in space; all weapons, ammo and keys when boarding), credits N, kill, win, lose, level N (jump into the space part of mission 1-10), board [N] (jump straight into the boarding level of mission N: 4 Courier, 5 Prison Barge, 8 Frost Command, 9 Bulwark, 10 Aegis Prime; without N the current mission), menu, quit. Up/Down recall earlier lines.

More flags: --script FILE.k2m plays a mission script; --coop N (0 to 2) with --board adds AI shield-brothers. Other programs in the package: server and netclient (LAN play), playtest (bot reports), texgen.

Stations#

F1 Helm#

Flies the ship. Throttle sets a target speed. With flight assist on, the ship damps drift; off, it is Newtonian. The Helm view accepts mouse fire. The autopilot steers around planets and moons.

F2 Weapons#

Lances (hitscan, heat), thunder torpedoes (slow, heavy), Gungnir missiles (homing, need a lock). Damage depends on the facing hit and on frequency matching.

F3 Engineering#

Reactor budget with power and coolant sliders for engines, warp, shields, lances, sensors and life support. Presets 1 balanced, 2 weapons, 3 shields. Heat beyond the limit burns a system out.

F4 Damage control#

System health and three repair teams you send to systems. Without life support you lose crew.

F5 Science#

Hold a scan on a target (N) to reveal its shield frequency, class and boarding difficulty. U runs a long-range sweep.

F6 Comms#

Hail (H), choose replies with the number keys, order wingmen, demand surrender, and read the message log.

F7 Map#

Sector map. Set a destination, add waypoints, start the autopilot, plot a warp to another sector (costs fuel; charge takes about 6 s at nominal power).

Crew AUTO#

Helm, Weapons, Engineering, Damage and Science can each run on AUTO at skill 0.7. AUTO is never as good as a focused player.

Sectors#

Mars Orbit (Mars, Forge, Phobos, Deimos), Phobos / Deimos (Phobos Relay), Asteroid Belt (Ceres, Belt Outpost), Jupiter / Ganymede (Ganymede Port), Luna (Tycho Fort), Earth Orbit (Concord forts, Geneva Dock). Warp gates are not built; warping between sectors uses the warp drive and fuel.

Starbases#

Friendly stations (Forge, Phobos Relay, Belt Outpost, Ganymede Port) offer repair, rearm, refuel, shipyard upgrades (levels 1 to 3, costs 400 and 900 credits) and a market of 12 commodities: ore, ice, iron, steel, grain, mead, medicine, electronics, weapons, machinery, luxuries and runestones. Prices vary by station (±30 %) and drift (±15 % every 90 s).

Boarding actions#

Levels#

Generated from a seed: a tile grid of 4 m tiles, rooms chained from the airlock, themed as corvette, station, prison barge or dreadnought bridge, in five sizes. Up to three doors on the main path are locked (bronze, silver, gold) and each key lies in a region reachable without it; a solver checks every level.

Starting kit and balance#

The boarding party starts with armour 40 and a scatter-gun (20 shells) as well as the axe and blaster. Monsters wake when they see you or hear gunfire within 22 m. Larger enemy ships hold more monsters (averages by size 1 to 5: 7, 14, 19, 27, 37) and every room carries an extra pickup. The numbers are in src/balance.rs; see Playtest bot and balance.

Co-op: shield-brothers#

On the objective screen press P to bring 0, 1 or 2 AI shield-brothers (the line P: SHIELD-BROTHERS (AI) shows the count). They arrive with you, shoot what they see, share keycards and the objective, and the monsters hunt whoever is nearest. The airlock only lets the party leave when every living member stands in it, and the action is lost only when everybody is down. The engine supports parties of up to four, so a human partner only needs an input source. The developer flag is --board 1 --size 3 --coop 2.

Monsters (hit points)#

Grunt 30 · Guard 100 · Gunner 175 · Medic 300 (revives corpses in 3 s) · Enforcer 240 (melee) · Mech 750 · Voss 1500. They see and hear you; an alarm calls everyone.

After a boarding#

The sabotage fuse is 45 seconds or the walk back to the airlock times 1.6 plus 20 seconds, whichever is longer, so deep levels stay winnable. Success: +15 credits per kill, +300 for the objective, +2 torpedoes and +3 missiles, and the ship is removed (captured or sabotaged). Crew lost equals damage taken divided by 25. Fleeing or dying ends the action; dying also ends the mission.

Mission scripts (.k2m)#

Missions beyond the campaign can be written as plain text and played without recompiling. Put *.k2m files in ~/.kagg2/missions/ (or $KAGG2_HOME/missions/); they load at start-up and are given mission ids from 11. --script FILE loads and starts one directly. Errors name the line. The example is missions/wolf-pack.k2m.

# comments start with '#'; strings are "double quoted"
mission "Wolf Pack"
sector belt                        # mars phobos belt jupiter luna earth
stage "FLY TO BELT OUTPOST" reach "Belt Outpost" 900
  say 1 "Sigrun" "Quiet out here."
  limit 400                        # seconds before the stage fails
stage "DESTROY THE WOLVES" clear
  spawn 3 player 0 0 -3500 attack interceptor,cutter label "Wolf"
  protect "Hauler" "THE HAULER WAS LOST"
stage "BOARD THE LEADER" board data Wolf
LineMeaning
mission "Title", sector NAMEtitle (optional); the sector the mission is flown in (required)
stage "TEXT" GOAL ...a stage: the HUD objective (under 80 characters) and its goal; the lines below it belong to it
throttle F, target, reach BODY RANGE, dock BODY, scan N, cargo COMMODITY QTY, upgrade, wait SECSgoals; commodity 0 is ore
clear, clear_but PREFIX, gone PREFIX, disabled PREFIX, escort BODY RANGEgoals; prefixes match ship labels
board OBJECTIVE PREFIXgoal: finish a boarding of the labelled ship; objective is sabotage capture rescue data boss
say AT "WHO" "TEXT"caption (under 100 characters) AT seconds after the stage starts
spawn AT ANCHOR X Y Z ROLE CLASSES [label "L"]anchor player (offset in the ship's frame, -z ahead) or body "NAME"; role attack patrol drone ally hauler; classes a comma list of interceptor cutter frigate destroyer dreadnought
protect "PREFIX" "MESSAGE", limit SECSthe stage fails with the message if a ship with that label prefix is destroyed; the stage fails after the time limit

Bodies named in a script are checked against the sector when it loads. Scripts have no briefing or ending; winning one simply ends the mission.

Multiplayer (LAN)#

One computer runs the server, up to seven players join and each takes one or more stations. Stations nobody holds stay on crew AI. The server owns the simulation, so players cannot cheat by editing commands: it only accepts commands for stations the sender holds.

cargo run --release --bin server -- --mission 2 --difficulty 1     # port 4747
cargo run --release --bin netclient -- 192.168.1.20:4747 Ingrid helm,weapons 60
cargo run --release --bin netclient -- 192.168.1.20:4747 Bjorn engineering,science 60

Server flags: --port, --mission N, --seed, --difficulty 0-2, --script FILE. The mission starts when every connected player has pressed ready (the text client readies automatically). The text client prints the objective and the flagship's hull once a second; a helm client holds 60 % throttle.

Limits: this is the headless layer only. The graphical game does not connect to a server yet, boarding and the dock screen are not available over the network (missions that need a boarding cannot be completed), and there is no encryption or login: use it on a trusted LAN only.

Playtest bot and balance#

Bots play the real game code: the bridge bot (sim/bot.rs) flies, fights, docks, mines, scans and asks to board; the boarding bot (board/bot.rs) walks a lock-aware path, shoots, loots and returns to the airlock. Capital ships are boarded with AI shield-brothers (one on size 3, two from size 4). Between campaign missions the bot spends its credits on upgrades.

cargo run --release --bin playtest                 # all missions x 3 difficulties, 3 seeds, then three full campaigns
cargo run --release --bin playtest -- 10           # the same with 10 seeds
cargo run --release --bin playtest -- 5 8          # mission 8, seeds 1 to 5   (KAGG2_DIFF=0..2, KAGG2_TRACE=1)
cargo run --release --bin playtest -- 12 boarding  # boarding win rates by theme, size and difficulty
cargo run --release --bin playtest -- bench        # sim timing

Latest results (4 seeds, each mission with a typical ship for that point of the campaign): missions 1 to 10 are won on every seed at Thrall, Karl and Jarl except a few boarding failures (mission 4 at Jarl: 3 of 4, mission 8 at Thrall: 3 of 4 when the bot sank Frost Command). Whole campaigns chained from mission 1: Thrall and Karl win all ten missions; at Jarl the bot loses the Courier boarding in mission 4. The bot is a cautious but unimaginative player: it never uses cover or dodges, so a human should find boarding easier at the same settings.

Solo boarding bot on Karl (12 seeds per cell): size 1 corvette-type levels 12 of 12 and bridge-type levels (capture or boss objectives) 7 of 12; size 3: 9 of 12 and 6 of 12; size 5: 1 of 12 and 0 of 12. At Thrall the figures are 12, 11, 3 and 11, 7, 0; at Jarl 11, 4, 1 and 6, 3, 0. That is why capital ships are tuned around a party.

Every tunable number lives in src/balance.rs: difficulty scale (0.7 / 1.0 / 1.4), boarding start kit, monster density, monster damage (0.25), hit-chance scaling, hearing range, extra pickups and the minimum sabotage fuse. The bots also found real problems that are fixed: a fleeing ship now escapes to warp once it is 9 km from every enemy (otherwise a chase never ended), mission 10's second squadron arrives after 45 seconds (the opening killed a fresh ship in 12 seconds), and the sabotage fuse scales with the level. Not covered: what feels fun. Real-time feel, sound and mouse control still need a human.

Performance and releases#

The sim costs about 2 to 5 microseconds per 60 Hz step in a space fight and about 20 microseconds in a size-5 boarding level with a party of three, against a frame budget of 16 667. On an Apple-silicon Mac the game averages about 10 ms per frame in space and boarding scenes (--shot out.png --frames N prints the average and worst frame time). The release profile uses thin LTO and one codegen unit.

tools/release.sh runs the licence audit, fmt, clippy and the tests, builds optimised binaries and packs dist/kagg2-VERSION-OS-ARCH.tar.gz (a zip on Windows) with the game, server, netclient, assets, these pages, missions and licences. .github/workflows/release.yml runs it on macOS, Linux and Windows runners (Linux needs the X11, GL and ALSA development packages). Only the macOS package has been built locally; the Linux and Windows builds are untested until the workflow runs. python3 tools/licence_check.py checks every dependency licence against GPL-2.0-or-later compatibility, checks that no shipped file is a copy of a reference file, and writes LICENCES.md.

Troubleshooting#

ProblemFix
cargo run says it cannot pick a binaryFixed: default-run = "kagg2" in Cargo.toml. The other binary is texgen (writes the texture PNGs).
Game is slowUse ./run.sh (release). Debug builds are slow because meshes are lit on the CPU.
Nothing can be boardedTarget the ship (T), get its hull under 60 % with shields gone, and close to 450 units.
A key does nothing after rebindingAnother action may share the key (red rows in Settings), or the key is used by a station panel.
Continue is greyed outNo save.txt yet: win mission 1 first.
A script mission does nothingThe console prints mod mission ...: line N: ... for parse errors; fix that line.
A client cannot joinCheck the port (4747), the firewall, and that both sides are the same version (the server rejects a protocol mismatch).
