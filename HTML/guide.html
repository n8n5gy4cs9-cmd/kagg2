Player guide

Getting started#

Kagg2 is a ship-bridge simulator with a first-person boarding game inside it. You fly a Marsgard longship, run its stations (or let your crew do it), cripple Concord warships and storm them. Ten missions take you from Mars to Earth.

  1. Run it. In the project folder run ./run.sh. It builds in release mode and starts the game. On an Apple-silicon Mac it is built for apple-m1 by default (.cargo/config.toml).
  2. Main menu. New Game starts mission 1. Continue appears once you have won a mission. Settings has difficulty, volume, mouse and key bindings. Help shows every key.
  3. Read the briefing and press Enter to launch. Esc returns to the menu.
  4. Follow the objective line at the top of the screen. Mission 1 is a tutorial: it asks for one thing at a time.
  5. Win by finishing every stage; you then see a debrief with a saga verse. Lose if your ship is destroyed, a protected ship dies or a timer runs out; the debrief says why and you retry the mission.
Tip: press F8 in space for help on the open station tab. The same texts are in the menu under Help.

Fighting in space#

  • Shields have four facings (fore, aft, port, starboard). Turn your strongest side to the enemy, and keep circling: a facing that is down stays down for a while.
  • Frequency matters. Scan an enemy (N, hold) to learn its shield frequency; set your lances to match it with [ ] for more damage. Change your own shield frequency with , ..
  • Heat. Lances heat up. Fire in bursts. Engineering coolant sliders cool systems; an overheated system burns out.
  • Power. The reactor budget is fixed. Presets on 1 2 3 in Engineering: balanced, weapons, shields.
  • Missiles need a lock (target first). Torpedoes are slow and heavy; best against open hulls.
  • Wingmen (green) follow orders from Comms. Hail ships with H; you can demand a weak ship's surrender.

Boarding#

A hostile ship can be boarded when it is disabled or crippled (hull below 60 % and shields almost gone) and you are within about 450 units. Target it and press B. In campaign missions that demand it, the objective is chosen for you; otherwise you choose.

ObjectiveDo
Sabotage the reactorReach the reactor, set the charges, escape to the airlock before the fuse runs out (at least 45 seconds, longer on big ships).
Capture the bridgeClear the bridge and use the console.
Rescue prisonersOpen the cells and free all three prisoners.
Steal the data coreTake the core from its room.
Kill Prefect VossBoss fight on the dreadnought bridge.

On the objective screen P brings up to two AI shield-brothers: they fight beside you, share keycards and draw fire, and the party leaves together at the airlock. Move with WASD, look with the mouse, fire with click or Enter, change weapons with 1–6, use things with E. Keys (bronze, silver, gold) open matching locked doors and always lie somewhere you can reach. When the objective is done, walk back to the airlock and press E. Dying ends the mission; Esc aborts and returns you to your ship.

Careful: destroy a ship you need to board (the Courier, the Prison Barge, Frost Command, the Bulwark, Aegis Prime) and the mission fails. Aim for the shields first and ease off once its hull is under 60 %.

Starbases and trade#

Dock at a friendly station to repair, rearm and refuel (the Forge is half price), buy upgrades and trade 12 commodities. Upgrades (to level 3): Rune Lances +30 % damage, Shield Generator +30 %, Reactor Core +20 % power, Hull Plating +25 %, Ordnance Bays +2 torpedoes and missiles per level. The mining bot (K on a targeted asteroid) fills your hold with ore.

Endings#

After Prefect Voss falls you choose: 1 Break the Armada (destroy the codes; an uneasy peace between equals) or 2 Take the Throne (use the codes; Mars rules). The choice is an epilogue and is saved.

Difficulty#

Settings → Difficulty: Thrall (easy, enemies 0.7×), Karl (normal), Jarl (hard, 1.4×). It scales enemy aim and aggression in space and the damage monsters deal when boarding. You can change it between missions.

Your own missions#

Missions can be written as small text files and played with --script FILE or by dropping them in ~/.kagg2/missions/; the format is in the manual. A LAN server lets several people share the bridge (multiplayer), but only as a text client for now.

Tips#

  • Cripple, do not kill: boarded ships give credits, ammunition and objective progress.
  • Dock between waves to rearm; docking also pauses the danger.
  • Let the crew run Damage Control and Science (AUTO) while you fly and shoot.
  • On big ships take both shield-brothers; the bots could not clear a size-5 dreadnought alone.
  • Mouse feel in boarding is a setting: lower the sensitivity if you overshoot.
  • If a key clashes with something, Settings marks both rows red.
